FAQs

What is ISHAVARYA ?

Ishavarya.com is a one-stop destination for curated festive and prayer-specific items for the household. We make shopping for puja and wedding essentials simpler for Indian families, while empowering local artisans and preserving cultural heritage.

Does ISHAVARYA provide complete puja kits ?

Yes. We offer festive kits such as Tulsi Pujan, Lakshmi Pujan, Govardhan Pujan and Dhanteras, so you get what you need for the occasion in one place. You can find them under Diwali Special Kits on the home page.

What product does ISHAVARYA offer ?

Our range covers pooja essentials such as diya, aarti book, kalava, swastika, ghee-baati, dhup, haldi, kumkum, zadu, ubtan and bukka, along with curated festive kits.

Does ISHAVARYA offer exclusive or premium products ?

Yes. Our Exclusive kits section brings together thoughtfully curated sets for special occasions. Visit it from the menu at the top of the home page.
